I KNELT BEFORE YOU,MAHADEV
VOICIN' THE SHEER WHISPER,
"ARE YOU THERE?"I QUERIED WITH SORROW.
SILENCE,I KNEW WAS YOURS."

"I FAILED EVERYONE".I SAID.
"MY HEART FEELS HEAVY,IT CARRIES
THE SATTERED DREAMS,MELTED HOPES.
YOUR ANSWER IS WHAT IT CRAVES'."

IN STILLNESS, I FIND
SOLACE AND CALM THAT ELUDES
ME FROM THE STORM THAT DWELLS WITHIN;
LIKE THE PAUSE BETWEEN TWO BREATHS.

"WHY MAHADEV?" I ASKED.
"MY PRAYERS WERE UNHEARD,IT SEEMS,
DID YOU NOT SEE THOSE WAKEFULL HOURS?
DID YOU NOT HEAR THE MUFFLED CRIES?"

AND THEN I FELT YOUR HAND
ON MY HEAD,A WHISPER
"CHILD,I HEARD EVERY SILENT LONGINGS.
I COUNTED EVERY TEAR."

"I STAND BESIDE YOU IN EVERY DARKNESS--
BUT TEMPEST RISE AND FALL,THROUH IT ALL
HOLD ON TO ME ,KEEP SOME FAITH
AND I'LL NEVER LET YOU FALL"

"SHIVA,I AM BROKEN".I QUIRRED
MY PLEAS SILENCED WITHIN.
"I DONT KNOW HOW TO STAND AGAIN"
ALL I DID WAS STIRRED.

"YOU DONT  HAVE TO STAND".HE SPOKE
"FALL,TOPPLE,CRUMBLE,IF YOU WANT
EVEN AT YOUR WORST ,YOU ARE MINE.
I AM THE DESTROYER AND THE MAKER
DO YOU THINK,I CANT REMAKE YOU?"

"IT WILL HURT".I SAID
"YES".HE SAID
"SO DOES BREAKING FREE"

SO I STAYED,
PLACIDLY SWAYED
BY FAITH.
NOT WITH ANSWERS,
NOT WITH STRENGTH,
NOT WITH TEARS,
BUT WITH HIM.
HOLDING ONTOME,
TO NEVER LETTING ME FALL.

"HAR HAR MAHADEV"
